# Proposed data and ledger contract for experiment EQ-001

Prepared 2026-09-06. This makes [FIRST-EXPERIMENT.md](FIRST-EXPERIMENT.md) concrete for implementation review. It is not an implemented engine, verified dataset or broker specification. Resolve sample findings and freeze both documents together before examining strategy returns.

## Information and identity

Each original file has a source URL/provider table, retrieval time, license scope, byte hash and schema version. Curated rows retain their source-file hash and transformation version. An experiment manifest names the code/environment versions, all input hashes, parameters, chronological partitions and attempted variants. Bulk files live in authorized durable storage; Git contains manifests, rules and reports.

| Record | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Security identity | Internal security ID distinct from issuer ID; source identifier; ticker/class/exchange effective intervals; historical classification and its source/availability evidence |
| Annual fundamental | Issuer, fiscal start/end, duration, currency/unit, original value, field mapping, accession/document; filing date; raw acceptance timestamp and timezone basis; public availability; vendor delivery evidence or declared lag assumption; retrieval time; revision identity |
| Daily price | Security, session/calendar, raw and adjusted field definitions, adjustment factors, original/reconstructed status, volume units, source/revision |
| Corporate action | Security, event identity/type, announcement/effective/ex/record/pay dates where applicable, split ratio, cash/share consideration, successor security, evidence and unresolved fields |
| Decision/order/fill | Decision cutoff and input manifest; pretrade position/cash; requested quantity and reason; simulated fill price/quantity/cost/time; rejection or residual; resulting position/cash/receivable |

Unknown and zero are distinct. All dates retain timezone/calendar context; an unlabeled timestamp is not silently assigned UTC. An observation period, filing date, acceptance time and retrieval time are different fields. Dated SEC filings can supply classification evidence where vendor metadata is only a current snapshot; availability must still be joined correctly.

Preserve company restatements and provider corrections separately when evidence allows. For historical reconstruction, a hash captured today freezes today's reconstruction; it cannot prove the vendor delivered that same row in 2005. For forward paper work, retain the actual input snapshot at every decision and never rewrite it. Where historic vendor delivery cannot be established, label the study as reconstruction from public information under a stated delivery-lag assumption, not a replay of the old vendor feed. A current `lastupdated` value alone does not recover older versions.

## Proposed accounting conventions

- **Reference capital:** $1,000,000 simulated starting NAV, solely a computational reference. This is not a proposed investment amount. All comparisons start with the same amount and zero positions. Whole-share orders and a 2% target cash reserve are proposed defaults; leave rounding residue in cash. No leverage, short positions or borrowing of dividend receivables. Capital-specific capacity and fixed research costs require a later study at the founders' actual budget.
- **Sizing:** Calculate desired target shares from the decision-time NAV and the latest available raw close; round buys down. Freeze requested orders before the next opening print. Process reducing orders before increasing orders with a deterministic security-ID ordering. Apply a simulated cash guard to reject an unaffordable remaining buy; log the requested order and budget rejection rather than retroactively resizing it to achieve a target weight. Excess cash stays in cash until the next scheduled rebalance.
- **Fill approximation:** The next session's raw opening print plus a stated per-side cost is a daily-data research benchmark. It assumes fillable volume and same-session funding from sales; daily OHLC cannot establish an attainable auction fill, actual queue order or settlement/broker behavior. The cash guard is a declared simulator convention. Inspect order size/lagged volume, missed orders and realized weights; a candidate needs calibrated execution/settlement assumptions and forward paper comparison before any trading decision. A missing or suspended open creates no fill; retain the position/order reason and reconsider at the next scheduled rebalance or documented corporate event. Never forward-fill a price into a trade.
- **Costs:** Charge `abs(filled_shares * raw_fill_price) * side_cost_rate` once for every buy or sell. Report the fixed 10/25/50 bp cases, order notional and turnover. Do not both worsen the fill price by that same cost and debit it again. Start cash earns zero in this first comparison; dividend receivables also earn zero. Returns are pre-tax and exclude standing data/compute subscriptions, which must be shown separately when evaluating dollar economics.
- **Splits:** Change held shares and per-share accounting units on the effective date without creating profit from the split. Mechanically convert frozen pending orders and reference prices into the new share units when a split or mandatory conversion occurs between decision and execution; this is not a fresh signal. For example, a pending buy of 10 pre-split shares at a $100 reference becomes 20 shares at a $50 reference after a 2-for-1 split. Reconcile fractional consideration where relevant. Share-class switches are a sale and purchase of different securities with trading costs, unless a documented mandatory conversion determines the exchange ratio. An issuer identifier must not collapse the two positions.
- **Dividends:** Record a receivable on ex-date for entitled shares held before ex-date trading, include it in NAV, and make it spendable cash only on payment. Payment converts the receivable into cash without recognizing a second gain. Do not reinvest before cash exists. The momentum total-return series can use its documented ex-date reinvestment convention; the held portfolio's cash ledger uses actual cash availability. These are different constructions. If payment dates are missing, flag the coverage gap and require a declared conservative cash-delay sensitivity; do not silently credit same-day spendable cash.
- **Other actions:** Cash mergers create consideration/receivables according to actual terms. Stock mergers and spinoffs create the correct successor security and quantities, including fractional cash where documented. Distinguish a known pending receivable, a new security, an exchange transfer and unknown terminal proceeds. Missing outcomes invalidate affected return claims until resolved or bounded transparently; a delisting flag alone determines none of these values.
- **Marking and reconciliation:** End-of-session NAV equals cash plus security market values plus action receivables, less any payable costs. Mark suspended positions separately with stale-price age and valuation status; carrying a stale mark is not evidence that it remains realizable. Retain daily positions and a reconciliation explaining every NAV change. No unexplained adjustment is allowed to make performance match a benchmark.

For Sharadar, the documented `open`, `close` and `volume` are split-adjusted and `closeunadj` is raw. A proposed reconstruction is `f = close / closeunadj`, `open_raw = open / f`, `volume_raw = volume * f`. Reject nonpositive/missing factors and test action dates against independent raw values. Keep source precision; use decimal currency accounting and a documented comparison tolerance derived from supplied price precision, not rounding to cents before returns. ## Meaningful implementation checks, once commissioned

The future implementation must pass known-answer cases for filing availability, a split, a dividend across ex/payment dates, a share-class switch, merger consideration, missing opens and insufficient cash. Compare the complete ledger from frozen inputs in independent implementations on a small fixture. This is necessary financial-accounting verification, not a test that merely copies implementation formulas.
