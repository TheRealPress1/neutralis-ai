## Finding

**The current entry recorder implicitly books a requested NO leg as BUY NO, irrespective of the actual venue action. It cannot faithfully represent a SELL YES execution or establish its inventory/basis consequences. Fixing execution quantity and price alone would preserve this error.**

There is an important venue distinction: the international path explicitly submits SELL on the token the code calls YES. The US path submits `ORDER_INTENT_BUY_SHORT`, which root-supplied official documentation defines as buying NO / long NO exposure. **US BUY_SHORT is not an unsupported SELL YES request, and BUY NO is an appropriate intended ledger side.** Its actual execution-price convention and acquisition basis still need evidence; the request-price convention alone cannot establish those. Do not treat the two adapters as having identical mechanics.

**International mapping is explicit.** `neutralis/engine/event_loop.py:2190` returns `clob_token_ids[0]` for either requested side and labels it the YES token. At `:2334` and `:2594`, a NO leg becomes `SELL`. Polling repeats the same mapping at `scripts/run_once.py:851` and `:866`; its comment asserting BUY NO equals SELL YES is an application assumption, not proof of economic equivalence. Directional polling similarly uses index 0 and SELL at `scripts/run_once.py:630` and `:646`.

The adapter passes that token and action unchanged into `MarketOrderArgs` at `neutralis/execution/polymarket.py:194` and `:217`. There is a related **request-unit mismatch**: these callers pass `leg_dollars` as `amount` even on SELL. The adapter documents SELL amount as shares at `:205`; installed `py_clob_client/clob_types.py:89` documents BUY dollars versus SELL shares, and `py_clob_client/order_builder/builder.py:103` constructs SELL maker quantity directly from that amount and taker consideration from amount times price. This establishes a source-level unit error; it does not establish the unknown response-unit contract discussed in the prior response report. Do not repair it by silently assuming NO dollars convert to sale inventory, or by silently changing to a different token.

**US mapping must remain distinct.** `neutralis/execution/polymarket_us.py:27` maps `no` to `ORDER_INTENT_BUY_SHORT`; `:218`/`:229` send that intent with USD `cashOrderQty`, and `:294`/`:305` do the same for a limit order. Neither request explicitly specifies SELL or a YES token. Installed `polymarket_us/types/orders.py:8` lists separate side and intent enums; `:70` declares returned `side`, `intent`, `price` and `avgPx`; `:95` declares execution `lastShares` and `lastPx`. `polymarket_us/resources/orders.py:24` passes the request through. None of those local declarations defines how BUY_SHORT translates into outcome ownership, price convention, collateral or source inventory.

Root supplied the current official [US orders overview](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/orders/overview) and [Python orders guide](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/sdks/python/orders), which resolve the intent interpretation: BUY_SHORT buys NO, and outcomeSide NO plus action BUY expresses the same request intent. The overview separately defines **request** `price.value` in the YES/long-side convention, illustrated by desired NO price 0.83 and submitted price 0.17. This supports the long-NO outcome mapping, but does not by itself establish whether execution `lastPx` or cumulative `avgPx` also use that convention, or provide actual NO acquisition consideration. An inferred complement of `lastPx` must not be treated as established merely because request prices are complemented. This review does not import the overview's looser economic-equivalence prose into international token accounting.

The US adapter then discards those execution details in `_normalize_result` at `neutralis/execution/polymarket_us.py:126`–`:179`. Event and polling callers reduce success further to order/ticker/status/venue (`neutralis/engine/event_loop.py:2122`; `scripts/run_once.py:976`). Thus the current recorder cannot validate a returned side, actual filled quantity or price convention. A future response reporting a SELL book-side must retain that field and original BUY_SHORT intent under its documented schema; neither dropping the field nor reclassifying the whole US request as an unsupported physical YES sale is justified.

## Smallest non-guessed M2 policy

1. **Separate desired outcome, submitted request and observed execution.** Bind the requested NO leg separately from the exact submitted token/slug, venue intent, actual buy/sell action when established, amount and its unit. Preserve returned side/intent, observed execution quantity/price/currency and raw evidence. US BUY_SHORT may be normalized to the documented acquisition intent for NO; its returned book-side and execution-price convention still need their own contract. Do not classify an expected venue-specific mapping as a simple response mismatch.

2. **Require an explicit supported projection before calling the existing entry recorder.** M2 may project a confirmed acquisition into BUY YES/BUY NO only when the source-supported contract identifies the acquired outcome, acquisition quantity and actual purchase consideration in the ledger's convention. An actual SELL YES observation must not create/add a BUY NO Trade/Position. Neither `price = 1 - sale_price`, requested NO price, negative quantity, nor the NO signal label establishes the necessary inventory, collateral and basis treatment. Adding enum values alone is insufficient.

3. **Keep unsupported executions visible without fabricating an entry.** Preserve an otherwise validated execution as observed, with projection unsupported and accounting application not attempted; leave the run unresolved and stop new submissions/continuation. Continue receiving already-outstanding responses. This is neither a rejected/no-fill observation nor zero exposure. Use the proposed existing-run evidence/outcome result, not a new parallel durable ledger. Do not mark the pair fully booked/hedged or calculate P&L from a surrogate BUY NO position. Within M2's proposed scope this retention is not durable recovery.

4. **Check projection capability before the first leg of a new live route.** If a required leg's submitted action/unit/economic projection is known to be unsupported, deny the selected route before sending any leg. This prevents knowingly opening a pair that cannot be represented, while point 3 still protects responses already in flight. International NO currently fails this check. For US NO, the acquisition intent is supported by root's official evidence; prove actual execution quantity and NO acquisition consideration/price convention before a positive BUY NO ledger acceptance test. Do not deny US NO merely because its intent name contains SHORT or its documented book-side differs from an outcome-action label. This is a narrowly scoped M2 proposal for the sole owner to accept, not a change applied by this review.

An alternative is a separately accepted expansion defining sale/inventory reduction, short/collateral cases and basis using venue evidence. That is larger than entry normalization. Deferring residual hedge conversion does **not** resolve initial projection. The minimum honest M2 acceptance must explicitly say which action contracts can reach the existing ledger and which remain preserved but unapplied.

## Small acceptance examples to add to the proposal

- Requested NO plus a documented SELL on the bound YES token: retain action/token/quantity/price, create no BUY NO trade or position, report projection unsupported and halt new continuation. Do not invent pre-existing holdings or short eligibility.
- Before submission, an international NO route using dollar allocation in a SELL share argument is denied before either venue is contacted; no heuristic conversion or token substitution.
- US BUY_SHORT is bound to the documented NO acquisition intent, separately retaining any returned book-side. A source-supported actual execution price/consideration convention permits BUY NO projection; missing execution-price evidence prevents basis application, and a request-price example or fabricated fixture flag cannot supply it.
- A supported BUY YES execution can be applied while a later unsupported SELL observation remains visible and unapplied; the overall run remains unresolved. Repeated callbacks do not create a substitute NO position, and already-outstanding observations are not discarded.
