# Optional independent research prompts

Prepared for Constantine and Gaspar, 2026-09-06. These prompts can be pasted into an external research chat if desired. ## 1. Historical-data procurement

We are building a private equities-research platform for two individuals, Constantine and Gaspar. We are evaluating daily/weekly stock strategies combining prices and fundamentals, with macro data where justified; no live trading or SaaS sales. Compare a small shortlist of data-source combinations suitable for reproducible backtests. Use primary vendor/regulator documentation and date every price/capability claim. Include SEC EDGAR, ALFRED and credible paid equities-history options. For each, distinguish original/as-reported from restated data; observation date from public availability date; active from delisted-security coverage; ticker from stable identity; and price adjustment from raw prices plus corporate actions. Verify history depth, bulk access, local/cloud storage, two-person use, retention after cancellation and external-AI processing rights where stated. Label unanswered license questions explicitly. Give budget options rather than selecting a subscription. Return a comparison table, exact sources, sample-data tests we should run before buying, and the smallest useful combination for a price-only pilot versus a credible fundamentals test. Do not imply that a modern historical API response is automatically point-in-time safe.

## 2. Candidate hypotheses and falsification

We are two individuals researching a new stock strategy within our internal platform Neutralis. We want a disciplined investigation over time before any real money. Propose at most three simple daily/weekly hypotheses with plausible economic mechanisms and limited data requirements. Prioritize primary academic evidence, independent replication and evidence of decay or failure. For each, specify the known-at-the-time inputs, universe eligibility, entry/exit/sizing policy, benchmark, relevant costs, overlap/dependence risks, likely failure modes and exact evidence that would reject the hypothesis. Explain how to limit multiple testing, freeze parameters, run chronological evaluation and gather prospective paper evidence. Distinguish pattern explanation from prediction. Address LLM knowledge leakage if text models are involved. Do not provide fabricated backtest results or guarantee returns. Recommend one initial experiment with explicit feasibility uncertainties, not a large autonomous-trading architecture.

## 3. Independent adversarial review

Review the attached research proposal and experiment artifacts as an independent skeptic. Do not trust the authors' performance summary. Identify the exact dataset snapshots, information-availability timestamps, strategy/code/model versions and every attempted variant. Check survival bias, future knowledge, revised fundamentals, corporate actions, return/cash bookkeeping, overlap between train/test observations, repeated use of the final test, missing transaction costs, unrealistic fill timing and benchmark risk differences. Explain whether any model-generated signals were evaluated prospectively. Return the three most material defects with exact evidence, then a verdict of insufficient evidence, rejected, or suitable for forward paper testing. Passing this review is not authorization for real-money trading.
