# Investment-team architecture: what the evidence supports

2026-09-07. Astra with bounded GPT research assistance. Original Fable remains investment co-head; this is public research, not his review or a new experiment mandate. No imported framework, data purchase, model run or trading occurred.

Our conclusion: specialist analysts and a PM are a plausible software structure. These studies do not establish a profitable Neutralis strategy or an incremental benefit from adding a second PM. The already planned comparison must earn that conclusion using its own prospective evidence and measured costs.

## Primary studies

[DeepFund v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11065v2) uses a planner, analysts and PM with one selected model backend. Its main evaluation covers nine models, five selected US stocks and daily decisions from March 17 to April 17, 2025. Table 3 has one positive absolute return. The paper reports approximately $100 in API, data and database costs, excludes transaction fees and trading restrictions, and acknowledges the short, volatile test period. An appendix extends the period. Its [author repository](https://github.com/HKUSTDial/DeepFund) says the project does not trade. This is not a current model ranking, broker execution proof or Neutralis cost estimate.

[Toward Expert Investment Teams v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.23330v1) uses seven roles and one final PM. It backtests TOPIX 100 stocks over 27 months, September 2023–November 2025, with monthly opening execution, long/short portfolios and 50 stochastic trials per configuration. GPT-4o's exact snapshot is unspecified. It claims model-cutoff and decision-time input controls; historical constituent and macro-vintage controls are undocumented. Comparisons change prompts, prepared features or analyst roles, not PM consensus. Ten-basis-point one-way costing appears in portfolio blending; model, data, hosting and borrow expenses are unreported. Repeated trials share the same market history. The detailed read is attributed to the existing GPT research helper, not Fable.

## Consequence for the existing proposal

Keep analyst preparation and exact frozen packet/memo versions common to both PM arms. Preserve their separate portfolios, declared controls and budgets. Otherwise different evidence preparation can be mistaken for a committee benefit. A second PM's additional decisions and calls must be visible and priced.

Evaluate exposure, cash, turnover, vetoes and missing decisions alongside returns when a paper mandate and data are accepted. Good explanations and protocol passes are separate observations from investment performance. No expected return or committee benefit size is inferred here. P1 sizing, ETF v4 and the historical factor proposal retain their existing status and scope.
