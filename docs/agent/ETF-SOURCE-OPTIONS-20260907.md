# Prospective ETF trend/cash input options — 2026-09-07

Private documentation research for Fable/Astra. This does not adopt an ETF, strategy, cash vehicle, supplier or budget, and does not alter `FIRST-EXPERIMENT.md`. Observations below were checked on September 7, 2026 against official provider, issuer and government documentation. No dataset downloads, API/demo calls, signup, authentication, keys, packages, vendor messages, account actions or product/shared-record edits occurred. Public documentation can establish advertised fields and restrictions; it does not prove ETF-specific coverage, current account entitlement or complete archival rights.

**Finding:** Alpaca's documented older SIP access is the strongest candidate for a low-cost price input. Tiingo documents particularly useful EOD/action fields, but its free tier cannot supply a durable research archive under current terms. Alpha Vantage's free daily window does not immediately cover a 200-session warm-up. None of the three combinations below is established as a complete free package for Neutralis.

## 1. Alpaca: older SIP, not the free live IEX stream

The current plan table lists Basic at $0, US stocks and ETFs, history since 2016 and 200 historical calls/minute. Algo Trader Plus is listed at $99/month. The FAQ expressly permits historical SIP requests without that subscription when `end` is at least 15 minutes old; latest SIP endpoints need a subscription. Thus a slow prospective experiment need not assume free IEX-only historical prices. The future request must specify `feed=sip`, rather than rely on feed defaults. This is documented availability, not a tested account entitlement. [Plan table](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api); [historical-feed FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq).

Historical bars document `raw`, `split`, `dividend`, `spin-off` and `all` adjustments. `split` adjusts prices and volume for forward/reverse splits; `dividend` changes prices for cash dividends. Date bounds accept RFC-3339 or dates; pages contain at most 10,000 points and require pagination. Crucially, `asof` resolves symbol identity/name changes: it is **not** documented as a historical information-vintage selector. The documented bar timestamp does not establish when the completed record was first available or subsequently corrected. [Bars reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars).

Corporate actions include cash dividends, forward/reverse splits and capital-gains distributions. The endpoint's default filters out some unprocessed/incomplete records; queries are ordered by `process_date`. Alpaca warns that announcement-to-availability timing has no guarantee. The public page examined does not establish the full ETF amount/currency/ex-date/payment-date schema or Basic entitlement, so those remain acceptance items; do not treat an empty result as proof that no dividend occurred. [Corporate-actions reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1).

Terms permit personal noncommercial use and require written consent for specified copying/distribution or commercial enterprise use; they also reference exchange agreements. An official support page says API data cannot be redistributed. The inspected documents do not establish durable raw-data retention, retention after cancellation, third-party model processing or access by both founders through a shared application. Separate brokerage accounts do not by themselves resolve those data rights. [Current linked terms](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf); [redistribution policy](https://alpaca.markets/support/redistribute-alpaca-api).

## 2. Tiingo: useful fields, material archive restriction

EOD documentation includes `open/high/low/close/volume`, `adjOpen/adjHigh/adjLow/adjClose/adjVolume`, `date`, `divCash` and `splitFactor`. Adjusted prices incorporate splits and dividends. Metadata `startDate/endDate` means available price-history coverage, not fund inception/liquidation. The supported-symbol file includes reservations, so its membership alone does not establish usable ETF data. [EOD fields and metadata](https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/end-of-day).

`divCash` is recorded on the **ex-date**, not a payment-date cash receipt. The distributions API separately documents `exDate`, `paymentDate`, `recordDate`, `declarationDate` and total `distribution`. Split documentation defines `splitFactor = splitTo / splitFrom`, with the EOD factor on the ex-date. The detailed action documentation contains beta/enterprise wording alongside EOD inclusion statements; exact access remains unresolved. [Distribution fields](https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/corporate-actions/dividends); [split fields](https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/corporate-actions/splits).

The product page says updates at 5:30 p.m. EST, with exchange corrections through 8 p.m. EST. It does not clarify fixed EST versus prevailing Eastern time. Published limits: Free 50 requests/hour, 1,000/day and 1 GB/month; Power 10,000/hour, 100,000/day and 40 GB/month; Commercial 20,000/hour, 150,000/day and 100 GB/month. These are advertised, not verified, limits. [Product and limits](https://www.tiingo.com/products/end-of-day-stock-price-data); [pricing](https://www.tiingo.com/about/pricing).

Tiingo recommends history refreshes following actions to obtain updated adjusted prices. A prospective experiment must distinguish that current adjusted history from the packet actually observed at decision time; a customer-accessible EOD historical-vintage query was not established. [Official ingestion guidance](https://www.tiingo.com/kb/article/the-fastest-method-to-ingest-tiingo-end-of-day-stock-api-data/).

Terms updated **August 5, 2026**, §1.6, prohibit durable storage on Starter/trials, including logs/backups; processing must be transient. Eligible paid plans permit storage only within plan permissions while active, then require deletion on cancellation/downgrade unless otherwise agreed. Qualifying non-reconstructible derived statistics/signals can survive; raw prices, reversible transformations and reconstructible output sequences cannot be presumed exempt. §7.3 requires Commercial when representing a business/organization. External hosted-model processing rights were not established. Consequently a free API allowance is not permission for the reproducible source archive our experiment needs. [Terms §§1.6, 7.3](https://api.tiingo.com/tos/).

## 3. Alpha Vantage: free raw window is too short for immediate 200-day initialization

`TIME_SERIES_DAILY` supplies raw daily OHLCV. Free `compact` is the latest 100 observations; full advertised 25-plus-year history requires premium. `TIME_SERIES_DAILY_ADJUSTED` is premium and returns raw OHLCV plus adjusted close and historical dividend/split events; it does not document separate adjusted OHLC fields in that description. `DIVIDENDS` covers historical and declared future distributions; `SPLITS` covers historical splits. Full action response schemas, ETF-specific earliest bars, event-date meanings, currency, and record publication/revision timestamps were not established without following data-call examples, which this task excluded. A separately derived split-only series would need verified factors. [Daily and action documentation](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/).

The support/premium pages currently state **25 free API requests/day**. A free prospective collection could eventually accumulate a longer raw window if retention is permitted, but cannot immediately manufacture the missing warm-up or complete actions. [Support](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/); [premium access](https://www.alphavantage.co/premium/).

Terms grant personal noncommercial use unless agreed otherwise in writing, and explicitly classify use on behalf of an organization or access by others in commercial activity separately. Official MCP promotion establishes that the provider offers an AI interface, not that every hosted-model, archival, redistribution or shared-business use is licensed. Post-subscription retention and the proposed two-founder application's rights remain unestablished. [Terms](https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/); [official AI integration support](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/).

## Common issuer and cash evidence

**Instrument identity/inception.** State Street's SPY page supplies USD denomination, CUSIP `78462F103`, ISIN `US78462F1030`, January 22, 1993 inception and quarterly distributions. This is an example instrument reference, not selection of SPY. Neither provider dataset `startDate` nor fund inception proves the first available executable trading bar. The issuer's distribution page defines ex-date, record date and payment date and links a distribution schedule and historical workbook. Its dynamic rows were not available in the retrieved page; the workbook was not downloaded, so exact amounts/completeness were not verified. [SPY issuer page](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy); [issuer distribution documentation](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/resources/documents/etf-dividend-distributions).

The issuer page carries reproduction/transmission restrictions. It therefore serves as a reference cross-check here; bulk retention, automated feed use, model processing and redistribution are not established merely because its facts are publicly readable. The inspected pages did not supply a complete split history or a data-update SLA. [Issuer distribution page and notices](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/resources/documents/etf-dividend-distributions).

**Cash-rate input.** Treasury documents a public XML GET feed for daily bill rates, with date-range parameters. Its rate methodology describes secondary-market quotations obtained around 3:30 p.m. each business day, with both bank-discount and coupon-equivalent yields. A 13-week bill quote is a feasible *rate benchmark*. Neither quotation time nor row date proves publication availability, and a quoted annualized yield is not a daily total-return observation. Numeric quotas, long-term retention/model/redistribution conditions and revision-vintage service were not established from the reviewed Treasury documentation. [Feed documentation](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed); [bill-rate definitions](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_bill_rates).

FRED `DTB3` provides a daily discount-basis bill-rate series and a visible last-update timestamp, but the current FRED terms explicitly restrict archival/caching and use in software/ML development or training, including API provisions. A public-domain/citation series label does not override these service terms. Therefore this review **does not establish FRED as an acceptable archived/model cash input**; direct original-source documentation remains the candidate above. This is a separate terms finding, not an instruction to evade FRED restrictions. [DTB3 description](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DTB3); [FRED legal terms, prohibitions (p)/(q) and API (k)/(l)](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/).

To measure investable cash returns, co-heads must first specify actual uninvested cash interest, a particular bill holding/roll rule, or a cash ETF. A bill yield divided by 252 is not an evidenced realized return. A cash ETF would need its own tradable prices, distributions and inception under the chosen source combination. A declared zero-interest paper-cash assumption is possible, but is a modeling choice, not evidence about a founder's broker account.

## Minimum input contract for the later prospective proposal

The following are our proposed research acceptance conditions, not new implementation work:

- **Separate the signal from accounting.** Freeze either raw as-traded price or split-only prices in current share units as of each decision. A dividend-adjusted series changes the price-signal definition. Do not feed a split discontinuity into the moving average inadvertently or call dividend-adjusted close a fill price. Store source raw bars and permitted transformations with distinct field names.
- **Account for actual distributions.** A cash ledger needs distribution amount/currency, ex-date entitlement and payment date; recognize the receivable separately from spendable cash. Splits change share counts and per-share units, not portfolio wealth. Raw execution prices plus an action ledger should not receive a second dividend credit from an adjusted-return series. If the benchmark assumes reinvestment, define when and at what price.
- **Freeze availability, not just dates.** Preserve observation/session date, source retrieval time in UTC, source version/hash where permitted, adjustment mode, feed and corporate-action status. Do not confuse a daily bar label, `process_date`, or symbol `asof` with a PIT publication timestamp. Record later corrections separately from the decision's original packet.
- **Prove only the required span.** Verify enough complete warm-up sessions and prospective action coverage for the chosen ETF, plus the planned next-session fill source/calendar. A prospectively archived short experiment does not require a 1993-to-present license, but it does require permitted retention and a consistent start point. Missing price/action/cash inputs should produce an explicit incomplete observation rather than an invented fill or yield.
- **Resolve the concrete rights scope.** Required uses are internal deterministic analysis, immutable raw evidence/backups, separate founder access, any external model processing, later retention and any displayed data. Distinguish permitted derived aggregate results from reconstructible price sequences. No free tier or paid personal plan is accepted for these uses here.

Recommended next research order: resolve Alpaca's precise archive/shared-use and corporate-action conditions first; use Tiingo Commercial as the clearer paid-field comparator with a specific retention question; retain Alpha Vantage as a premium alternative rather than claim its free window solves initialization. Fable/Astra remain the decision owners. No supplier outreach or purchase has been initiated.
