The current research and historical experiment have different minimum inputs. For prospective company analysis, one original filing plus cited contemporaneous events may be useful without 25 years of prices. For portfolio decisions and paper outcomes, the frozen mandate additionally needs reliable prices, actions, identity, benchmark conventions and rights. For historical factor evaluation, original vintages, dead securities and terminal outcomes are indispensable. Buying a news feed or enabling a current broker quote does not fill those historical gaps.

The near-term engineering contract should therefore carry explicit evidence coverage and deny a decision when its required inputs are missing. Hashes establish byte identity, not truth, publication time or data entitlement. A packet can be valid as a synthetic protocol test while remaining unfit for an investment experiment; those statuses must remain separate in output.

## Economics to measure

Using Constantine's stated $20 Railway plus $25 Supabase only as an illustrative planning amount gives $540/year before data, model, tax and trading costs. If all $45/month were allocated to equities, that equals 10.8% of $5,000, 5.4% of $10,000, 2.16% of $25,000 or 1.08% of $50,000 annually. These are arithmetic expense ratios, not predicted returns or a capital recommendation. At $100/month total expense the same $10,000 capital has a 12% annual fixed-cost ratio.

Use both incremental and allocated cost views; do not charge the same expense twice. Net strategy evidence must beat an appropriate same-risk simple alternative by enough dollars to justify its additional cost, rather than merely show a positive market-year return. Model costs should be measured per actual role/cycle, with caching as a subset of input. Do not buy continuous infrastructure because the development team happens to be multi-agent.
