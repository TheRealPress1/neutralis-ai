# Co-head discovery inputs for the Robinhood investment-team pilot

Version RH-DISCOVERY-1, 2026-09-07. Astra's integrated discovery reference following Fable's independent peer proposal and complete correction-review PASS. These are questions the connection specialist must answer, not an accepted experiment, approved budget, selected data feed or live mandate. The separate historical factor experiment in FIRST-EXPERIMENT.md remains unchanged.

The co-heads own the experiment. The proposed smallest comparison is one PM with software controls versus two independently briefed PMs with identical software controls, using shared research and separate paper portfolios, plus passive benchmarks. Different model families are a proposed diversity measure; they do not guarantee independent errors. A sequential-challenger variant is an optional later comparison. Proposed workload is 30–50 liquid US large caps, a fixed initial universe selection rule and weekly decisions. A short initial feasibility window evaluates data quality, factual accuracy, costs and process; it cannot establish investment profitability. No dates, limits, model rates or minimum capital are accepted from this reference.

For EACH input below the RH lead must give a dated, evidence-linked coverage verdict: public source with verified access conditions; documented Robinhood capability dependent on later onboarding; or missing/unverified. Name actual documented field granularity, freshness, history and licensing limits rather than extrapolating tool names. A publicly visible price is not proof of a usable licensed historical feed. Static computation and internally specified records must be distinguished from vendor-supplied facts.

| ID | Required input or boundary | Coverage questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I01 | Candidate universe and security identity | Dated eligible list, listing/share class/security identifier, ticker changes; contemporaneous volume history sufficient for a fixed selection rule. A fixed prospective universe is not a historical survivor-free universe. |
| I02 | Decision-time market prices and liquidity | Daily raw OHLCV, timestamps/timezone/calendar, availability time, corrections and reliable symbol/security joins; sufficient trailing observations for computed returns/volatility/liquidity. |
| I03 | Paper execution observations | Next eligible session's open or another explicitly proposed execution convention, availability and gaps, halts and unavailable prices; an absent observation cannot become a fabricated fill. No broker paper-account assumption. |
| I04 | Corporate actions and cash | Splits, dividends, effective/ex/payment dates, mergers/delistings and share/cash consideration, adjusted-versus-raw price convention. Double counting or invented terminal proceeds must be avoided. |
| I05 | Company financial evidence | Original filing accessions, acceptance/publication time, fiscal period, currency/units, revenue, profit, balance sheet and cash-flow evidence; distinguish later restatements and incomplete metrics from then-available facts. |
| I06 | Company events | Earnings/guidance/filing dates and attributable event evidence with publication and retrieval times, missing coverage and usage rights. A future calendar estimate is not a known future event outcome. |
| I07 | Industry/macro context | Minimal reproducible sector/exposure information and dated public macro observations; releases and revisions separated. One cached cycle memo is a proposed workload, not one call per company. |
| I08 | Benchmarks and diagnostics | Compatible total-return or explicit cash/dividend series for the fixed-universe passive portfolio and broad index reference; comparable timing, cash/exposure, turnover and cost conventions. Correlated stocks and overlapping horizons are not independent trials. |
| I09 | Frozen research packet | Versioned source snapshots/rights, cutoff and retrieval time, coverage gaps, calculations, prompt/model/config IDs; no new browsing during the decision step. This is our internal provenance contract, not a Robinhood API field. |
| I10 | Paper portfolio and mandate | Separate arm holdings/cash/receivables, identical explicit limits and execution assumptions, timestamped decisions and approvals. Proposed objective exit rules must name observable triggers and unknown-data handling; no live automatic exit authority follows. |
| I11 | Cost and quality records | Per-role and per-arm call counts, bounded follow-ups, input, output and cached-prefix tokens, with cached counted as a subset of input (uncached = input − cached) and current chosen-provider rates when available, data fees, incremental and allocated hosting cost. Pre-outcome decisions, rejections and counterfactual limitations remain visible. |
| I12 | Eventual broker boundary | Real account identity, token/consent scope, account/positions/order/fill schemas, custom-client feasibility, tool entitlement and limits, disconnect/refresh, single execution owner including other desktop agents. These are later onboarding-dependent questions, not authorization to make calls now. |

Quant measurements should be computed in code. A combined company fundamentals/catalyst memo and one macro memo per cycle may cover the named analyst functions economically. Neither analysts nor PMs receive unrestricted account data or order tools. Joint approval applies to every discretionary rebalance; a proposed objective risk-exit rule belongs to an explicit paper mandate first, with any future live policy separately settled.

The specialist must identify the smallest subsequent OWNER-performed onboarding steps and the evidence each would establish, without performing those steps. Inspect only official support/product pages and exact public well-known discovery document URLs retained in RH001. Do not request `https://agent.robinhood.com/mcp/trading` itself, registration/authorization/token endpoints, or initialize/list/call financial MCP tools. A well-known metadata document explicitly pinned in RH001 remains permitted even if its URL path ends in `/mcp/trading`; it is distinct from the forbidden financial endpoint. A tool's order-preview simulation does not establish a complete broker paper sandbox; do not claim a verbatim no-paper statement unless an actual primary source supplies it.

Acceptance remains a complete docs-only capability memo, with every I01–I12 covered and a narrow future interface/file map against exact RH001. No model experiment, dataset purchase, infrastructure change, authentication or live trading is commissioned by this input reference.
